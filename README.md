PROJECT OVERVIEW

The DeKUT Campus Navigation System is a proposed mobile application designed to help students, staff and visitors find their way around the main campus of Dedan Kimathi University of Technology (DeKUT).

We chose this idea because navigating a large university campus can sometimes be difficult, especially for new students and visitors. A user may know the name of a lecture hall, department or facility but still not know exactly where it is or which route to take.

Our goal is to make campus navigation simpler by allowing users to search for locations, view important campus buildings and facilities, and get clear route directions.

PROBLEM STATEMENT

DeKUT main campus has several buildings, lecture halls, hostels, cafeterias, access gates, parking areas and other facilities spread across different parts of the campus.

For someone who is unfamiliar with the campus, finding a specific location can take time. Students may have to ask other people for directions or walk around trying to identify the correct building.

The problem is particularly noticeable when looking for a specific lecture hall inside a large building such as the Resource Centre.

The proposed system addresses this problem by providing a simple mobile interface for searching locations and following directions.



The main users of the proposed system are:

New students – students who are still learning the campus layout.

Continuing students – students who need to find unfamiliar lecture halls, departments or facilities.

Staff members – staff who need to locate offices and campus facilities.

Visitors – people visiting DeKUT who may not know the campus layout.


Main Features

1. Campus Map

Users can view a map of the campus and identify important locations.

2. Search Location

Users can search for buildings, lecture halls, departments and facilities.

3. Route Directions

Users can select a starting location and destination and view route information, distance/time and simple turn-by-turn instructions.

4. Buildings Directory

Users can view a list of campus buildings and identify their categories and locations.

5. Department Location

Users can find a department, office location and floor information where applicable.

6. Lecture Halls

Users can view lecture halls, their capacity and building location, then request directions.

7. Facilities

The facilities section includes:

Library

Cafeterias

Medical Centre

Banking services

Sports complex

8. Parking & Transport

Users can find:

Parking areas

Bus stop locations

Taxi pick-up points

9. Emergency Services

Users can access information about:

Security services

Health services

Emergency contacts

Nearest emergency locations

USER RESEARCH

During the project, the group identified campus navigation as a challenge that can affect students who are unfamiliar with certain parts of the university.

The group also experienced several design challenges while developing the Balsamiq prototype:

One member worked on choosing suitable routes to represent in the prototype.

Another member focused on ensuring that different possible routes were considered.

Another member worked on representing the lecture halls within the Resource Centre.

These challenges helped us understand that a navigation system needs to present routes and locations in a simple and organized way.

USABILITY TESTING

The prototype is intended to be tested with at least five representative users.

Example tasks include:

Find the Resource Centre from the Home screen.

Find a lecture hall and open its directions.

Find the Medical Centre using the Facilities screen.

Find a building using the Buildings Directory.

Start a route from a selected gate to a destination.

During testing, the group will record:

Whether the participant completed the task.

Errors or difficulties experienced.

Comments from the participant.

Suggestions for improvement.
